What it does

Four actions, always ranked

Attack, Use Ability, Defend, and Retreat are scored every time, so you always see the full picture, not just one suggestion.

Every score explained

Offensive Value, Survival Risk, Resource Efficiency, and Positional Advantage each come with a plain-language reason, generated by the same deterministic rules, not an AI guess.

Optional Game Master's take

Claude narrates the top recommendation in flavorful tactical language. It explains the ranking; it never changes it.

An original ruleset

A generic combat model inspired by tabletop RPG mechanics. Not affiliated with, and doesn't reproduce, any specific published game.

Why it matters

Mid-combat, it's easy to default to "just attack" every round, even when defending, repositioning, or disengaging is the smarter play. Having the same four options scored the same way, every round, makes the tradeoffs visible instead of automatic. This one's also here to show a different kind of build than the other two tools: the same deterministic-scoring, AI-narrates philosophy, applied somewhere with room for personality.

How we're different
